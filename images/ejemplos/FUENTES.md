# Fotografías de referencia

Fotografías de ejemplo de Unsplash; no son fotografías del negocio. Se usan localmente como respaldo visual mientras el administrador carga sus fotos propias.

`pizza-combinada.webp` es una imagen generada con la herramienta integrada `image_gen` el 1 de noviembre de 2026. Muestra una sola pizza, mitad champiñones y mitad hawaiana; se exportó a WebP de 900 × 900 para la carta.

Prompt utilizado: “Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: restaurant pizza menu product photograph, square image, no text. Primary request: One single round whole pizza divided into exactly two clear semicircles: the left half topped with abundant sliced button mushrooms and melted mozzarella, the right half Hawaiian pizza topped with golden pineapple chunks, ham and melted mozzarella. Both halves must be visually distinct on the SAME pizza, with a clean center dividing line, appetizing golden browned crust. Realistic Colombian pizzeria food photography, overhead to slight three-quarter angle, pizza fills about 85 percent of the square frame, centered on a simple dark wooden serving board, neutral warm backdrop, soft natural light, crisp believable food texture. No people, no hands, no extra pizzas, no lettering, no logos, no labels, no split-screen, no collage. This image will be the first product in Don Pieros Pizza's existing menu.”
